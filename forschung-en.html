Research Focus Areas

My main interest lies in the territories of the former Holy Roman Empire of the German Nation, with a focus on southern Germany, as well as the formerly German-settled territories such as Breslau (Wrocław), Prague, Brünn (Brno), Danzig (Gdańsk), Königsberg (Kaliningrad), Stettin (Szczecin), Posen (Poznań), Reval (Tallinn) and Riga, as well as the Hanseatic cities of Lübeck, Hamburg and Bremen and their significance beginning with the late Middle Ages, as well as the families connected with their history.

Alongside the great houses, my interest has also fallen in particular on less prominent merchant, patrician, official, scholarly and officer families. They often held a formative position in their surroundings over a long period, but their genealogical record and their history in general have today regrettably in many cases been entirely forgotten or lost. To this came the wars and the devastating effects of the wars, especially of the last two World Wars, which destroyed an immense amount of German cultural heritage and whose victims included several important archives.

In our time, digitalisation has created the first widely usable medium which, it is to be hoped, will survive future crises and fires better, so that an end can be put to the loss of sources.
