Genealogy and Heraldry

This website is intended to serve the research results of my past work and, above all, to bring closer the subject areas that went along with this work.

Since the beginning of the Covid pandemic I have not pursued any new research. Through this website, however, I would like to continually add topics I have worked on in recent years, as well as to publish research suggestions and comments. They are intended to encourage independent research and promote scholarly exchange.

The focus of my work lies in the territories of the former Holy Roman Empire of the German Nation. My interest is not only in the well-known and frequently researched families, but also in families whose traces in the sources are hard to find today, as well as questions of noble and estate law.

Countless websites on many of the topics dealt with here already exist; nevertheless, it is important to me to take up some of them again from time to time and also to shed light on critical points of research.

As my technical knowledge is rather limited, I am always glad to receive suggestions and ideas for improving this website, which is still at an early stage.

Heinrich Türkewitz
